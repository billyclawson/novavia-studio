Reference-led portrait photography for iPhone. Follow framing and pose guidance while you compose your next shot.

PhotoCoach / Visual study

THE IDEA

A reference is just
the beginning.

PhotoCoach is in development. A public release date has not been announced, and no download is currently available.

  1. 01

    Start with a reference

    Choose a starter reference or select your own image to guide the portrait you want to make.

  2. 02

    Compose with guidance

    Use a translucent reference overlay and pose guidance to help place your subject in the frame.

  3. 03

    Capture and review

    Capture photos, review them in the app and choose the pictures you want to export.
