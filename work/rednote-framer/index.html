Inside RedNote Framer

A closer look at the app.

01

Start with the original

Import a photograph from Photos or Files. Read its camera and exposure information from EXIF.

02

Make the crop yours

Drag and pinch to compose the photo. Move the divider to balance the picture and the space around it.

03

Choose the shape

Work in 3:4 or 3:5 layouts and adjust the camera placement within the composition.

04

Keep the camera in the story

Use camera information from the photograph, with automatic matching and a manual selection option.

05

Room to change your mind

Undo and redo while you edit, and restore your current work when you return to the app.

06

A JPEG, ready to go

Inspect the result, save it to Photos or open the native share sheet. Export removes source GPS metadata.
