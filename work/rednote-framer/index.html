RedNote Framer

A considered iPhone workspace for cropping, framing and exporting your photographs.

RedNote Framer / Visual study

THE IDEA

Give the picture
its own space.

RedNote Framer is in development. It is a project of Novavia Studio and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Xiaohongshu / RED. A public release date has not been announced.

  1. 01

    Bring your own photograph

    Import an image from Photos or Files and work from the selected source image.

  2. 02

    Find the right composition

    Adjust the crop and frame, with undo and redo while you refine the layout.

  3. 03

    Export when it feels right

    Create a JPEG, save it to Photos or share it using the iPhone share sheet.
