Two focused tools for different parts of making a photograph.

PhotoCoach

In development · iPhone

A reference in mind.
A little help in frame.

Turn “a photo like this” into a starting point. Choose a reference, follow framing and pose guidance, then take the picture at your own pace.

  • Your own reference, or a built-in starting point
  • Assisted and solo shooting modes
  • Single shots, 3-shot and 5-shot sequences

RedNote Framer

In development · iPhone

Give your photograph
a proper finish.

Bring in a photo, shape the crop and build a frame around it. Keep the camera and exposure details close, then export a JPEG ready to share.

  • Import from Photos or Files
  • 3:4 and 3:5 layouts with hands-on crop controls
  • Undo, redo, save and share
